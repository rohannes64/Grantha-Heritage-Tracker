About this project

A student project for Smart India Hackathon problem SIH26096: group unindexed heritage scans by topic without labels, and let a new page be checked against those groups.

Where the books come from

The National Digital Library of India gathers records from many libraries; the scans behind many of them come from the Digital Library of India, whose copies are served by the Internet Archive. The sample was drawn from that collection with a fixed random seed, an equal share per language and per era, published up to 1960 or undated.

Five pages are taken from inside each book, away from covers, contents and indexes, and read with Tesseract 5 in the book's own script.

Collection
digitallibraryindia, Internet Archive
Languages
English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Bengali, Gujarati, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil
Pages per book
5, at 15, 30, 50, 70 and 85% of the way through
OCR
Tesseract 5 with orientation and script detection
Methods
Hierarchical clustering (Ward, average, complete), DBSCAN, PCA, silhouette

Rights

The catalogue marks these scans' rights as not available. The project keeps only the text it read, the catalogue details and links back to the archive. Page images on this site are shown by link from the Internet Archive and are not copied or redistributed.

What it cannot do

  • Read handwriting: the archive's "manuscripts" here are printed books.
  • Give a book a subject heading: groups are patterns of shared words, named after their most distinctive ones.
  • Promise a group is right: the study page reports how stable each grouping is and where it is unsure.
  • Read Urdu: Tesseract reads its script poorly, so it was left out of the sample.
