What is inside the archive?

The Digital Library of India holds scanned books in many Indian languages, and its catalogue rarely says what a book is about.

The archive’s own subject field turned out to record where a book was scanned, not its topic. So the study reads the pages and groups books by what they say.

The archive as a map

Every book is a point. Books whose pages read alike sit close together, whatever script they are printed in.
